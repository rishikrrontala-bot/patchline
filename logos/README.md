# Patchline logo concepts

All six SVGs are transparent, font independent vectors using the Patchline paper/ink/orange design world. The full logos use path outlines based on the site's Helvetica Neue Bold wordmark with matching tight tracking. The orange dot continues the existing punctuation treatment. The icons remain legible at small sizes and include accessible titles and descriptions.

1. **Signal patch** — preserves the familiar tilted frame and three-channel construction while giving the middle signal a clear focal role.
2. **Evidence loop** — a continuous source-to-review path, with orange markers at the beginning and inspectable endpoint.
3. **Quality gate** — three incoming channels terminate at a prominent gate, reflecting the product's quality hold behavior.

Colors: paper `#F4F1EA`, ink `#151A18`, signal orange `#CE5B3A`. SVG backgrounds are transparent; place on paper or another suitably contrasting light surface.

The two `study-*.png` files are early image-generated explorations. Use the SVGs for final branding; they remain sharp at any size.
