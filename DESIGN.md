# Patchline design world

## Intent

Patchline should look like a clinical instrument under examination: precise, calm, and candid about uncertainty. The hero is the *working signal-to-decision loop*, not a decorative product render. It borrows editorial spacing and fine rules from a research folio and the fast readability of a monitoring console. Every simulated element is visibly identified.

## Palette and type

Paper `#F4F1EA`, deep ink `#151A18`, cool gray-green `#667670`, signal orange `#CE5B3A`, warning red `#A74236`, rule `#C9D0C9`. A muted green is for sound data, orange for changing patterns, red for data faults. No purple gradient, glow, shadow-heavy cards, or fake clinical photography. Display type is a restrained serif; measurements and provenance use a monospace face. Text stays legible at phone width.

## Composition

One large patch silhouette explains the four channels. Synchronized traces occupy the center of the screen. A single decision rail shows the score, quality state, and hypothetical treatment visualization. The handoff reads like a short lab note, with every quantitative claim linked to samples. Hairline rules organize information; empty space distinguishes observations from interpretation. Print view becomes a plain one-page report with provenance and limitations.

## Motion and interaction

Scenario changes may animate the trace reveal briefly, but the numbers and state never depend on animation. Buttons show selected, hover, focus, and disabled states. Clicking a handoff claim highlights the underlying samples. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement. Keyboard users can reach every control and evidence link.
